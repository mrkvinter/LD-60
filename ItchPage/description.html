The old laboratory wing has been sealed for years. Now it's your job to put it back in order. No one has set foot here in a long time, but you're the one who has to clean it up. Why? That's for you to find out. Along with why your fingers look so... appetizing.

Assignment
Clear the building of clutter and put everything in its proper place.
Large items go on the pallets; small ones go on the shelves.
Resources are stored separately, by the entrance.
Some of the furniture has been destroyed. Repair it with whatever resources you find in the lab.
WARNING! UNDER NO CIRCUMSTANCES ARE YOU TO REPAIR THE TELEPORTER.
— Management

Your Shift

  • Sort items onto the shelves and pallets
  • Build and repair with the resources you find
  • Upgrade your abilities
  • Read the notes left behind
  • Don't repair the teleporter

Controls

Controls
[WASD] Walk
[Mouse] Look
[LMB] Pick up / Put
[RMB] Take from shelf
[G] Throw
[TAB] Upgrade
